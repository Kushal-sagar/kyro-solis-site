The Problem We Solve

Advanced Materials Are Hard to Adopt. We Make Them Straightforward

The performance, safety and compliance of a modern product are increasingly decided by its materials. Yet the advanced materials that solve these problems are specialised, hard to specify, and largely imported into India with long lead times and little technical support. Most suppliers sell parts from a catalogue, not solutions. We exist to close that gap.

Performance Is Now a Materials Problem

Heat density, fire safety and electromagnetic interference set the limits of what EV, electronics and industrial products can achieve. The right material is often the difference between a product that ships and one that does not.

Compliance Is a Gate to Market

Safety and EMC standards decide what can be sold, and to whom. Missing them does not just fail a test, it closes a market and slips a programme.

The Supply Chain Is the Missing Piece

These materials are specialised and largely imported, sold without engineering support. Adoption is slow, risky and dependent on distant suppliers. A structured domestic partner changes that.

Thermal Protection

Aerogel Composites

Ultra-thin, flame-rated insulation that contains heat and stops thermal runaway from spreading. It fits where conventional insulation is too thick, and it keeps products on the right side of the safety rules that decide market access.

Thermal conductivity as low as 0.015 to 0.021 W/m·K UL94 V-0 flame rating From 0.5 mm thick Stable beyond 600°C

Typical values. Application-specific values are confirmed through sampling and testing.

Applications

EV Battery Packs Energy Storage (BESS) Industrial and Rail

Thermal Management

Graphene Thermal Films

Heat-spreading films that pull heat away from dense, high-power electronics, so devices run cooler, faster and longer. They manage the thermal load that limits how small and powerful a product can be.

In-plane conductivity up to 1,500+ W/m·K From 10 µm thick 400,000+ fold cycles Around 4x lighter than copper Stable to 500°C

AI Compute EV Power Electronics Consumer Electronics
